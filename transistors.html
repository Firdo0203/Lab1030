Transistors

Main I/O devices Memory Transistors

Transistors were an important development in the advancement of computer machinery. Earlier computers used large electronic components called vacuum tubes to process electrical signals. These components required a lot of power, produced heat, and took up a large amount of space. A transistor today is a semiconductor device used to work on electrical signals. It uses at least three terminals for connections to electronic circuits. A small electrical signal can control a larger flow of current, allowing transistors to amplify signals and act as electronic switches. They are commonly found on computer chips to regulate electrical current through the chip parts. This is considered one of the 20th century’s greatest inventions.
